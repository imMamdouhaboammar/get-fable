# Skill Authoring Standard

## 12-Section Canonical Anatomy
Every Fable skill must adhere to the 12-section standard:
1. **Purpose**
2. **When to Use**
3. **When NOT to Use**
4. **Inputs**
5. **Expected Outputs**
6. **Procedure**
7. **Decision Rules**
8. **Tool Policy**
9. **Evidence Requirements**
10. **Failure Handling**
11. **Completion Criteria**
12. **Neural Connections & Referring Links**

## Progressive Disclosure Standard
- Level 1: Frontmatter metadata (~100 words).
- Level 2: `SKILL.md` body (<500 lines).
- Level 3: Bundled resources (`references/`, `templates/`, `examples/`).
