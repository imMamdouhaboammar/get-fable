# Kiro Fable 5 Rules

Kiro AI assistant governance powered by **get-fable**.

## Protocol

- **Route Jobs**: Match task shapes to the 14 canonical lifecycle skills.
- **Card Discipline**: Keep active cards in `.fable/LEDGER.md`.
- **Verify Mutations**: Do not mark tasks done without verifying the latest workspace mutation.
- **Recover Deterministically**: Enter `fable-recover` after repeated errors.
- **Spark Guidance**: Align with `get-fable spark` for next-action clarity.
