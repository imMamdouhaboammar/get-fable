1. **Domain-Specific Meaning**: Define terms as they function in this project or architecture, not general dictionary definitions.
2. **Ambiguity Prevention**: Distinguish similar concepts (e.g. `evidence` vs `observation`, `phase` vs `gate`).
3. **Discoverability**: When a solution introduces or relies on a key term, index it here to establish shared vocabulary across agents.
